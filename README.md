FlexAura Home Physio UK Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
FlexAura Home Physio UK is a UK-based home physiotherapy platform connecting patients with qualified physiotherapists who provide treatment at patients' homes, relatives' homes and care homes. The platform offers a range of services, including musculoskeletal physiotherapy, sports injury rehabilitation, neurological physiotherapy and post-surgical recovery.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning FlexAura as an accessible, trustworthy and convenient home physiotherapy service. It prioritises the home-visit offering, communicates practitioner credentials and pricing more clearly, and creates a more straightforward journey from discovering a service to booking an appointment.
2. Main Improvements
1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Content Hierarchy
- Gave the home physiotherapy offering greater prominence in the hero section.
- Strengthened the headline and supporting messaging to explain the platform's main value proposition.
- Organised physiotherapy services into clearer categories.
- Improved the visibility of key customer benefits.
Problem addressed: The previous homepage presented numerous services and information without sufficiently prioritising the main home-visit offering, making the business purpose less immediately clear.
2. Stronger Trust and Credibility Presentation
- Improved the visibility of HCPC registration and practitioner verification information.
- Organised professional credentials, DBS checks and insurance information more clearly.
- Strengthened the presentation of service standards and customer trust signals.
Problem addressed: Important practitioner credentials and verification information were not communicated clearly enough, potentially creating uncertainty for patients considering home visits.
3. Clearer Booking Journey and Conversion Structure
- Improved the visibility of appointment booking calls to action.
- Presented published consultation and home-visit pricing more clearly.
- Structured the journey around selecting a service, finding a practitioner and arranging an appointment.
- Made key booking information easier to access.
Problem addressed: The previous booking journey lacked clear connections between service selection, local availability, pricing and appointment arrangements.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Established a modern, professional and reassuring appearance aligned with FlexAura's patient-focused healthcare positioning.
- Homepage Layout: Improved content hierarchy to prioritise home visits, practitioner credibility, services and pricing.
- User Experience: Simplified service discovery and created a clearer path towards finding and booking physiotherapy.
- Content Consistency: Presented services, practitioner qualifications, verification information and pricing in a more structured format.
- Conversion Focus: Increased the prominence of booking prompts and essential decision-making information.

Project Focus: Stronger home physiotherapy positioning, clearer practitioner credibility and a more straightforward booking experience.
